MK3 Rear Wing

Decided on an airfoil shape for the rear wing of the MK3 race car. Currently solving how to mount the rear wing to the main chassis.

Summary

Decided on the EP423 airfoil for the rear wing of the MK3 race car. While S1223 has generally higher Coefficients of Lift (CL) for each angle, E423 had better L/D ratio. EP423 also had better performance consistency at lower angles of attack. Xfoil viscous solutions for Re = 4e5.
